A FEW LITTLE MOVES

Think it. Gesture it.

  1. 01
    Pinch to draw

    Bring thumb and index finger together. Release to lift your pen.

  2. 02
    Pinch to erase

    Touch thumb to middle finger. Your middle fingertip becomes the eraser.

  3. 03
    Stretch your creation

    Pinch with both hands. Spread to grow, bring together to shrink, or twist to rotate.

  4. 04
    Give it a little nudge

    Choose Move, then pinch near a stroke and drag it into place.

Let go to rest. Losing your hand stops drawing automatically.

Fine-tune your setup

Use even lighting and keep your full hand in view. Lower smoothing follows faster; higher smoothing steadies small movements.
