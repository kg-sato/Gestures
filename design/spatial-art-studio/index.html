GESTURES / QUICK GUIDE

A little space to make something.

01 / Sculpt with a mouse

Click a form to select it. Drag the coloured handles to move, rotate or scale its whole assembly. Adjust its shape, dimensions, colour and finish in Object. Detach before reshaping a connected part.

02 / Look around

Drag empty space to orbit. Scroll to zoom; right-drag to pan. Reframe resets the view. Turntable slowly rotates the view for a presentation.

03 / Put your hands in it

Choose Live camera, connect your webcam, then pinch thumb and index over a form. Add a second pinch to resize and tilt. Open to release; amber connectors and a ghost show a magnetic snap. Brief hand loss holds position, then safely releases.

04 / Give it motion. Keep what you make.

Play physics lets objects float, collide and toss. Gravity pulls them onto the virtual grid. Paused physics still lets you sculpt. Export a scene to edit later, a GLB model to use elsewhere, or a PNG of the composition. Camera images stay on your PC.
