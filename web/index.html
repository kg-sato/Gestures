◎
Pinch to grab

Touch thumb and index over an object. Hold briefly, then move. The amber part selects the whole connected assembly.

↔
Two hands

Keep the first pinch held and pinch with your other hand. Spread to resize; tilt the line between your hands to rotate. Release either hand to keep moving with one.

⌁
Release to toss or attach

Open your pinch to throw. Near a free connector, amber points and a ghost show where it will attach on release. Collisions alone never attach.

⊞
Build your assembly

Depth moves the selected assembly closer or farther. Detach separates the amber part. Gravity pulls onto the virtual grid. Freeze pauses objects while the camera stays live. Add up to 12 parts; Reset restores four.
