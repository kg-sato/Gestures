Meet your jelly.

Your camera shows through the jelly. Shape it with your hands.

  1. Move: point just your index finger at a piece, hold briefly, then drag. Pieces bump and push each other.
  2. Stretch: pinch the jelly and pull. Use two pinches to stretch a piece wider.
  3. Slice: open index + middle fingers like scissors, then close them over the jelly.
  4. Squish: bring two open palms together. Or push one open palm toward a screen edge.
  5. Make a ball: close your hand around a smaller sliced piece.

Make deliberate movements. Each action gives your camera a different comic filter. Up to eight pieces; reload for a fresh jelly.
