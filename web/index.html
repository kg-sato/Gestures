DONUT LAB / CONTROLS

Pinch. Stretch. Twist.

01 / One pinch reshapes the tube

In Live camera, hold thumb and index together anywhere in view. Slide up to thicken, down to thin. Choose Ripple to make the ring wavy instead. Release your pinch to keep the shape.

02 / Two pinches open and twist

Add your second pinch. Spread your hands to open the hole; bring them together to narrow it. Tilt the line between your hands to twist the oval tube. Release one pinch to return to one-hand sculpting.

03 / Let tracking settle

The hand markers show where tracking sees your pinch. Hold it briefly to start. If a hand disappears, the shape holds still; tracking resumes with a fresh baseline so it does not jump. Keep hands separated and well lit.

04 / Mouse and mesh

Sculpt mode: drag horizontally to open the hole and vertically to change thickness or ripples. Orbit mode: drag to turn the view; scroll to zoom. Sliders edit the same surface. Show topology reveals the triangles; Detail changes their density. Undo restores a whole sculpting gesture.

05 / AR Catch

Choose AR Catch and pinch near the small donut to pick it up. Flick your hand and open your pinch to throw. Pinch with your other hand near its flight path to catch it. Hold the receiving pinch ready; a green circle marks its catch window. Tracking loss never throws. In Studio, drag and flick the donut with a mouse to try the flight.
